# 总序卷第2章来源笔记

## 来源 1：Norbert Wiener, Cybernetics or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine

- 来源链接：<https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/4581/Cybernetics-or-Control-and-Communication-in-the>
- 用途：作为第2章理论起点，说明 CHS 的“控制与通信”语言并非凭空出现，而是继承了控制论关于反馈、信息与系统边界的历史源流。

### 可直接支持的关键表述

> With the influential book *Cybernetics*, first published in 1948, Norbert Wiener laid the theoretical foundations for the multidisciplinary field of cybernetics, the study of controlling the flow of information in systems with feedback loops, be they biological, mechanical, cognitive, or social.

> At the core of Wiener's theory is the message (information), sent and responded to (feedback); the functionality of a machine, organism, or society depends on the quality of messages.

### 本章写作含义

该来源可用于支撑第2章开篇的历史锚点：CHS 不是对传统控制论的抛弃，而是把“控制—通信—反馈”这套基本语言，带入现代水系统运行对象的重构之中。它还能帮助说明，控制论原本就不只面向机器，也面向生物、认知和社会系统，因此 CHS 把对象扩展到物理、信息与组织层，具有理论继承性。

## 来源 2：Cyber-physical systems in water management and governance

- 来源链接：<https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877343523000374>
- 用途：支撑“水管理对象已经广泛进入 CPS 阶段，且需要进一步纳入社会与生态维度”的判断。

### 可直接支持的关键表述

> Cyber-physical systems (CPS) are increasingly common across the water cycle.

> CPS are transforming how we manage and govern water safely and sustainably.

> Cybernetic approaches can enable social and environmental dimension inclusion in CPS.

> Water governance is facing rapid transformations as cyber-physical systems (CPS) are deployed across water-related sectors and river basins.

> We then focus on how smart technologies connect to people, policy and ecosystems. Key to our argument is that integrating the social and ecosystem dimensions into CPS research and design will be vital for sustainable transformations in water management and governance.

### 本章写作含义

该文可以支撑第2章中的一个关键判断：在水管理实践中，对象边界已经不可能停留在“物理设施 + 自动化设备”的狭义定义上。随着 smart technologies 进入流域和多部门治理场景，people、policy、ecosystems 必须进入对象语言。这说明 CHS 既可以借鉴 CPS/CPSS 的框架，也必须给出更适合水系统运行闭环的专门对象定义。
